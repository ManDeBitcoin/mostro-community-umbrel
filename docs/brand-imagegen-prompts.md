# Prompts de la propuesta de marca

Se usó el generador de imágenes integrado (`image_gen`). La referencia de estilo fue el [logo de Mostro](https://github.com/MostroP2P/mostro/blob/main/static/logo.png); el archivo oficial no se introdujo como imagen de entrada. El usuario seleccionó después las variantes finales: `assets/logo.png`, `assets/logo-ambient.png` y `assets/icon.png`.

## Logotipo: generación inicial

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: final horizontal logo concept for Mostro Community Manager, a Bitcoin P2P community operator dashboard.
Primary request: Create an original, carefully designed logo inspired by the visual DNA of the official Mostro mark: energetic angular green geometry, a lightning motif, strong dark outline, and a hint of the pointed silhouette. Reinterpret these cues as a mature, distinctive emblem for an operator tool. The emblem should suggest an M and a connected community node through balanced negative space. It must remain recognizable at 32 px. Avoid a cartoon monster, face, eyes, mascot, or a copy of the official logo.
Composition: one centered horizontal lockup on a genuinely transparent background, emblem on the left, wordmark on the right, generous padding. Exact text on two lines: "MOSTRO" and "COMMUNITY MANAGER". The top word is large and bold in a considered condensed geometric sans; the descriptor is clearly legible and secondary. Render every letter exactly once and spell MOSTRO M-O-S-T-R-O and COMMUNITY MANAGER C-O-M-M-U-N-I-T-Y space M-A-N-A-G-E-R.
Style: crisp flat vector-like graphic, deliberate geometry, precise spacing, strong silhouette, polished open-source Bitcoin software brand.
Palette: electric green around #64D985, very dark forest #10241B, off-white only if needed for contrast. Two or three flat colors maximum.
Constraints: no gradients, shadows, glow, texture, 3D, mockup, border, background scene, watermarks, extra words, Bitcoin symbol, or generic shield icon.
```

Make the lettering readable: set the word MOSTRO in solid off-white #EDF4EF with bold condensed uppercase geometric type, and set the exact subtitle COMMUNITY MANAGER in solid mint #9EDAB3. Keep the text on two lines to the right of the emblem. Replace the black gradient/glow background with one completely uniform flat dark forest color #0C1110. Remove all glow, blur, shadows, gradients and texture from the emblem and text. Verify exact spelling: MOSTRO; COMMUNITY MANAGER. No other objects or words.
